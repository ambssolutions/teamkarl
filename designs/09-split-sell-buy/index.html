About Karl

Born and raised in the Eastern Beaches

Proudly born and raised in the Eastern Beaches, Karl is your trusted Howick-based real estate expert. His devotion to real estate has resulted in over 25 years of experience, and close to $845 million in property sales.

Tap into Karl’s local expertise and strong connections in Howick and East Auckland. As one of Harcourts’ leading global agents, Karl stands out for his stellar track record and dedicated customer base.

Away from work, Karl lives on a Whitford lifestyle block with his family and dozens of rescue animals among 20,000 predominantly native trees — and loves NZ surf spots and travelling Europe & Asia.
