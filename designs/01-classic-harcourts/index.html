About Karl

Your trusted Howick-based real estate expert

Proudly born and raised in the Eastern Beaches, Karl's devotion to real estate has resulted in over 25 years of experience and close to $845 million in property sales. Tap into his local expertise and strong connections in Howick and East Auckland.

As one of Harcourts' leading global agents, Karl stands out for his stellar track record and dedicated customer base.
