Scene 03 Meet Karl

Born and raised in the Eastern Beaches.

Proudly born and raised in the Eastern Beaches, Karl is your trusted Howick-based real estate expert. His devotion to real estate has resulted in over 25 years of experience, and close to $845 million in property sales.

Tap into Karl's local expertise and strong connections in Howick and East Auckland. As one of Harcourts' leading global agents, Karl stands out for his stellar track record and dedicated customer base — including the award of Top Harcourts Consultant in Howick.

Home is a Whitford lifestyle block, shared with family, dozens of rescue animals and 20,000 predominantly native trees.

When he's not selling, you'll find Karl at New Zealand's surf spots, or travelling through Europe and Asia.
