Why I do what I do

Top 5 Harcourts agents in New Zealand — 5th year in a row. Awarded Top Harcourts Consultant in Howick.

Kia ora, and thanks for stopping by.

I'm proudly born and raised in the Eastern Beaches, and Howick has always been home. My devotion to real estate has given me over 25 years of experience, and along the way I've been part of close to $845 million in property sales — for neighbours, old school friends and thousands of families across East Auckland.

Those years have given me something you can't get from a textbook: local knowledge and strong connections right across Howick and East Auckland. I'm grateful to be one of Harcourts' leading global agents, and even more grateful for the clients who keep coming back and recommending us to the people they care about.

Away from work, you'll find me at home on our Whitford lifestyle block with my family, dozens of rescue animals and around 20,000 trees — most of them native. When I get the chance, I'm chasing waves at a New Zealand surf spot, or travelling through Europe and Asia.

If you're wondering what your home is worth, or simply where to start, give me a call. There's no pressure — just an honest conversation.

Karl
